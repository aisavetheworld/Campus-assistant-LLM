Cost of Optometry Exams and Products

Schedule your comprehensive eye exam by logging onto MyStudentChart > Click on visits > Schedule an appointment > Choose Comprehensive Eye Exam

To place an order for glasses/contacts, call the Optometry Department at (858) 534-2602 . You may also walk-in during normal business hours to place your order. If you have questions, email us at SHS-Opto@health.ucsd.edu .

Undergraduate and graduate students who are enrolled in the Blue View Vision Plan (Anthem) receive significant discounts on exams and products. Those with UC SHIP or RAFT are eligible for discounts only at the UCSD Student Health Services Optometry Clinic. To find out more details about your vision plan, please visit UC SHIP or call Anthem Blue View Vision at (866) 940-8306.
