Student Conduct Policies and Procedures

Before posting, you must visit your ResLife office to receive a stamp of approval.

All postings that do not have the ResLife stamp of approval or are posted in non-approved areas will be removed.

No door-to-door soliciting or promoting of events is permitted, including when posting on apartment and suite doors (“door tagging”).

All areas not listed as approved posting locations are prohibited, including but not limited to: walls, doors, or within elevators.

Please contact your Residence Life office for information regarding approved posting locations.
