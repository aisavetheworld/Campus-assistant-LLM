Advising for New F-1 and J-1 Students

This page has a friendly link that's easy to remember: iNewStudentWebinars.ucsd.edu

The International Services and Engagement Office (ISEO) is excited to welcome you to UC San Diego! ISEO supports F-1 and J-1 students with immigration-related advising questions. Please Note: For assistance with housing, tuition, booking classes , health requirements , or other university services, please contact the appropriate campus office.

ISEO’s Pre-Arrival Webinars are your go-to resource for all your questions about becoming an F-1 or J-1 student and beginning your journey at UC San Diego! Register for upcoming webinars to participate in the live Question & Answer sessions. Come back to review webinar recordings and slides as much as needed.

Still have immigration questions?

If you still have questions related to your F-1 or J-1 immigration status and are unable to find the answer in the resources above, please email ISEO and we will be happy to assist you. Please be sure to include your UCSD email address and your PID or Triton Student Number.
