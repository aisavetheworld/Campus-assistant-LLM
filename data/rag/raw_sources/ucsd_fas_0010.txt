Applying for financial aid is the first step toward accessing the money you need for your educational journey. Meeting the cost of attendance is a significant investment for many students, but UC San Diego is committed to providing a comprehensive need-based aid program that makes it financially possible for all students to attend our university.

Ready to take that first step? Check out the links below for full details on applying for aid.

Undergraduate Students

Current or prospective undergraduates must submit a FAFSA or CA Dream Act Application each year in order to receive financial aid. Discover more about how to apply, important deadlines, special cases like benefits for military-connected students, and more.

Graduate Students

Graduate students receive primary funding through their academic department. If graduate support is limited, students may be eligible to apply for student loan options via the FAFSA or CA Dream Act Application. Learn more about applying, loan types, eligibility requirements, and more.
