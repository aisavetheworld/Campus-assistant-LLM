HDH Undergraduate Neighborhoods

Welcome to the HDH Undergraduate neighborhoods! We’re excited to have you as part of our community of Transfer and Upper Division students across the Pepper Canyon East and Matthews Apartments, Pepper Canyon West Living Learning Neighborhood, the Rita Atkinson Residences, and Pangea Residence Halls & Apartments! Residents within our spaces are from all eight colleges at UC San Diego, and we strive to make UC San Diego your home.

Four Complexes, One Community - Upper Division Housing The Upper Division Housing neighborhoods are home to four vibrant residential communities: Pepper Canyon East & Matthews Apartments, Pepper Canyon West Living Learning Neighborhood, the Rita Atkinson Residences, and Pangea Residence Halls & Apartments. Each community offers a unique living experience and is located in different areas of campus, yet we operate as one connected team. No matter where you live, you’re invited to participate in events, activities, and programs across all four communities, fostering a strong and inclusive residential experience.

Experience the best of campus living! See what Upper Division Housing has to offer and explore everything our vibrant residential community provides. More information is located below.
