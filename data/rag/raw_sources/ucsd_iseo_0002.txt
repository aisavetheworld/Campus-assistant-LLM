Financial Information Review

When a scholar submits the "J-1 Initial Financial Information and Submission" eform, a second approver email is automatically sent to you (the departmental contact) with an embedded link to the "Funding Information Review" eform. Check your inbox or an email titled: "ACTION REQUIRED: Review Financial Information."

Confirming the scholar's funding sources: The scholar eform requires them to upload all documents pertaining to any funding, aside from USCD paid appointments; however, ONLY departmental contacts will input numeric funding values.
