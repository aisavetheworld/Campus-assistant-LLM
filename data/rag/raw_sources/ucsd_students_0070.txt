How to Check Your Grades

Learn how to check your grades for last quarter and for previous quarters.

Check your grades for last quarter:

To see last quarter's grades: You can check your grades 10 days after finals using the Academic History Tool . Check the notice posted above your grades to see when your grades will be finalized.

Don't see a grade for your class? Check the Late Grades Report to see classes for which grade reports have not yet been turned in, then contact your instructor.

Check your grades for previous quarters:

To see grades from past quarters: Use the Academic History Tool and scroll down the page.
