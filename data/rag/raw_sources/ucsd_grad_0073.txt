New Employee Orientation for Academic Student Employees (ASEs) and Graduate Student Researchers (GSRs)

The Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs (GEPA), in collaboration with Labor Relations and the UAW, hosts quarterly New Employee Orientation for  Academic Student Employees (ASE) and Graduate Student Researchers (GSR).

These are campuswide orientations for NEW employees within the Academic Student Employee (ASE) and Graduate Student Researcher (GSR) units who are covered by the collective bargaining agreements between the University and the UAW. During this orientation, the UAW will be provided 30-minutes to present to new ASEs and GSRs without the presence of University officials.  The campuswide orientation is in lieu of participation by the UAW in any department-wide or hiring unit-wide orientation.

All ASEs and GSRs who have not previously attended an orientation must participate.

Orientation Information

The University and the UAW have scheduled sessions across 3 days. New ASEs and GSRs are required to attend. They only are required to attend one session.

ASEs or GSRs with questions about the UAW ASE-GSR Orientation may refer to the schedule below or connect with the UAW directly: sandiego@uaw4811.org .

ASEs and GSRs should complete the following RSVP form and select the Orientation that matches their job title: RSVP Form . If they have already attended a UAW Orientation, please indicate that on the form (last question on the form).

ASEs and GSRs should only attend the session that corresponds to their job title (ie. Tutors/Readers or TAs/GSRs). If a conflict prevents attendance at the correct session, they may attend another session.
