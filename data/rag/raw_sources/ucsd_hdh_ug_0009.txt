UC San Diego’s first college aims at developing well-rounded students prepared for competition in a complex world. Structured curriculum develops essential skills, such as creative writing and thinking, using the language of science and scholarship as well as the actual art of learning.

Neighborhood & Housing

First-year Revelle students are housed in a cluster of eight multistory residence hall buildings overlooking Revelle Plaza. Revelle offers triple and double rooms , with limited single room availability.

64 Degrees, Umi, and Roger’s Market are all adjacent to student Housing. Basketball courts, a green roof space (with ocean view), and the Main gym and Natatorium swimming complex are also within a short walk.

Named for the renowned naturalist and wilderness preservationist, John Muir College is deeply committed to supporting humane goals and purposes. Self-sufficiency and individual choice are integral to the college’s philosophy of learning.

Neighborhood & Housing

Located on the west side of campus within walking distance of the beach, Muir residential housing surrounds a grassy quad where students can study, play frisbee, or socialize. First-year students are generally housed in Tenaya and Tioga high-rise residence halls featuring suites of primarily double and triple rooms and some singles . Second year students get the opportunity to select space in Tuolumne or Tamarack apartments.

Opposite the Housing complex are Pines and Roots restaurants , Redwood Lounge, a small coffee shop, and a convenience store. The Mandeville Center for the Performing Arts, a pool, gymnasium, recreation fields and volleyball, basketball and tennis courts are nearby, making it possible to live a rich and varied life within a small area and maintain a strong community spirit.

Roosevelt College (ERC)

Eleanor Roosevelt College (ERC) honors one of America’s most noted political leaders and humanitarians of the 20th Century. ERC emphasizes a comprehensive general education that prepares students to compete in the global and multi-cultural economy of the 21st century.

Neighborhood & Housing

The majority of first-year students live in residence hall suites of primarily double and triple rooms and some singles , bordering a community green and Cafeé Ventanas.  Apartment spaces may be available as well with mini-double rooms and some singles. Ventanas, The Village Market, and The Bistro Dining facilities are nearby, as well as the RIMAC sports complex.

Laundry facilities, study and meeting rooms, mailroom, game room, student activities center, student-run TV station and International House are all conveniently located in the ERC neighborhood. RIMAC campus recreation facility is just a short walk away.

Sixth College at North Torrey Pines Living Learning Neighborhood

Sixth College challenges students to explore the historical and philosophical connections between culture, art, and technology. Creativity, imagination and artistic expression are encouraged as students learn to take intellectual risks and think outside the box. The residential life program serves to enhance the academic mission and philosophy of the college by making connections to learning experiences outside the classroom.

Neighborhood & Housing

Most Sixth College first-year students live in predominantly double and triple rooms (with some singles) housed in 3 buildings in the new home for Sixth College at North Torrey Pines Living Learning Neighborhood.  Shared suite lounges and kitchenettes are available for studying and socializing. More information on our new home is available on the Living at Sixth website . Dining at Sixth Restaurants are nearby.  The living area is conveniently equipped with wireless internet.

Continuing students for Sixth College live in apartments—furnished with kitchen, living room and offering a mix of single, double, and triple bedrooms .

Laundry facilities and mailroom are available in the neighborhood.

Earl Warren College honors the former U.S. Supreme Court Chief Justice, who believed that responsible citizens take charge of their lives. Students are encouraged to connect their education with personal and career goals.

Neighborhood & Housing

Residence halls feature suites of triple and single rooms overlooking the UCSD Ecological Reserve. Freshmen may also be housed in apartments, located on the opposite side of Canyon Vista and Earl’s Place eateries. Each two bedroom apartment houses four or five students in double and triple rooms .

Warren Housing offers covered bike storage and laundry rooms, JK Wood 24/7 study lounge, Amazon Locker, Student Activities Center (SAC), Canyon Vista Marketplace and Earl’s Place ( the largest halal-certified residential dining facility in the nation with a menu that features healthy, sustainable menu offerings and groceries. Stations include Fusion Grill, Fresh, Three-Sixty and Earl's Coffee House. ). Residential buildings surround basketball and sand volleyball courts. Canyonview Aquatic Center with an Olympic-size pool, climbing wall and weight room is close by.

Eighth College has a mixture of residence hall and apartment style units.  Eighth College is located next to Revelle College on the southwest side of campus.  A mixture of double, triple and some single rooms will be available. More to come as the community comes alive this Fall!

Theme of Eighth College

The Engagement & Community theme is both timely and persistent. It is at the heart of some of the most difficult challenges that humanity faces, worldwide: structural racism; widening disparities in economic wealth, health and wellbeing; climate disruption; ecological degradation; the globalization of chronic and infectious disease types and vectors; and the declining resilience in the capacity of human settlements and working lands to adaptively respond to increasingly frequent and devastating shocks happening worldwide. Importantly, the Engagement & Community theme leverages and is aligned with campus-wide efforts to combat structural racism and anti-Blackness.

Marshall College at Ridge Walk Living Learning Neighborhood

Thurgood Marshall College’s philosophy emphasizes scholarship, social responsibility, and the belief that a liberal arts education should include an understanding of each person’s role in society.

The newly designed apartments for Marshall College at Ridge Walk Living Learning Neighborhood (RWLLN) will have housing for approximately 2,400 students adjacent to Hopkins Parking Structure and occupying the old TMC Lowers Apartments.  Apartments will house 8 or 10 students per unit. The new Pepper Canyon West neighborhood includes two towers with 3 outdoor terraces per tower and large top-floor lounges with expansive views of campus and the surrounding La Jolla area. Each tower also boasts two large laundry rooms and 4-5 study room / lounges per residential floor, broken up into larger and smaller spaces to accommodate groups or those in need of more quiet space. Ground floor projects include 4 welcome lounges, a 5,800 sq. ft. food service / retail space, a 3,500 sq. ft. game room, and a 1,900 sq. ft. fitness facility.

All students will be in single-occupancy bedrooms in 6- or 8-bedroom shared apartments with a kitchen, spacious living room and bathrooms. Priority will be given to transfer students. Pepper Canyon West has a 12-month contract . A Dining Plan is not required, and the neighborhood is within convenient access to public transportation.

Incoming transfer students who meet eligibility criteria will be able to express a preference for the 12-month housing contract in Pepper Canyon West. Contracts will be issued based on availability. Otherwise, incoming students will receive the standard 9-month contract for housing at Pepper Canyon East, Matthews, and Rita Atkinson Residences.

Neighborhood & Housing

Residence halls house students in suites that vary of single and double rooms surrounding a spacious common area with a living room, large bathroom, and a patio. Apartments house students in 5 or 6 person units with a single room, double room and/or triple room.  Buildings are two-story and close to Pangea parking structure and the newly built Ridge Walk Living Learning Neighborhood.

OceanView , laundry facilities, meeting rooms, game room and student activities center are located nearby. RIMAC campus recreation facility, and Geisel Library are just minutes away.
