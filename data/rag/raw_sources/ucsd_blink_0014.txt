Schedule of Classes: Standard Teaching Times

If you create the Schedule of Classes for your department, college, or program, refer to this list of standard teaching times when you schedule classes.

Use the standard teaching times below to assign days and times to classes in the Schedule of Classes.

Facts about teaching times:

Classes requested for standard teaching times will be assigned to classrooms first.

If you don't choose a standard teaching time, the class will start at 5 p.m. or later, based on available space.

Discussion sections and other 50-minute classes meeting on Tuesdays or Thursdays follow the standard teaching times for MWF.

Classes scheduled in campus classrooms and lecture halls will start at :00 or :30 and end at either :20 or :50.
