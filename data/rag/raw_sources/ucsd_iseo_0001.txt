H-1B/E-3/TN Extension Requests

Employment Based (H-1B/E-3/TN) Extension and Amendment Requests

H-1B status may be extended in three-year increments up to a total of six years. We recommend departments submit H-1B extension requests 7 months in prior to the anticipated start date.

Please allow considerable time for this process as extension/amendment applications take as long as initial applications.

Substantive changes in H-1B employment must be received by USCIS with an amended petition submitted prior to the change in employment circumstances. This requires a new recharge and USCIS filing fees. Substantive changes include:

Moving between represented and nonrepresented positions or between different represented positions, from research to clinical care; or adding a work-site outside of the metropolitan statistical area e.g. Postdocs teaching in summer will require an amendment and potentially multiple H-1B amendments to add and remove teaching duties.

e.g. Postdocs teaching in summer will require an amendment and potentially multiple H-1B amendments to add and remove teaching duties.

A normal stepwise progression within an employment series (e.g. Assistant Project Scientist to Associate Project Scientist) will not require an amended petition

A change in worksite location, may require an amendment or a new NOI posting, please consult with an ISEO advisor.

Employment authorization is retained with a timely filed extension or amendment petition; the petition does not have to be approved by USCIS until 240 days into the new H-1B period.

H-1B Actual Wage Rate and Submission

The actual wage rate is that wage paid by the employer to employees in similar positions. "Employer" means UC San Diego as a whole, and not just a sponsoring department. Therefore, it may be necessary for you to obtain salary data for individuals outside your immediate department. Please work with your MSO or equivalent if you do not have UCPath access to this data.

When establishing a salary range for a job category, please compare employees in similar positions; if you don't have multiple positions for comparison from department level data, please expand the comparison to include school level data

Example: if your department only has one Bioinformatics Programmer 3 employee, please expand your salary range to include all Bioinformatics Programmer 3 employees in the entire school

Do not include the prospective H-1B's salary within the salary range (low and high) that you are establishing

Identify any job-related reasons for why this employee is being placed where they are within the overall range; such factors might include extensive publication record, education (PhD whereas other employees in the position have baccalaureate degrees), years of experience in the field, etc.; i f you include "Other," please indicate the additional rationale, such as "granting agency terms require payment at this level"

The above rationale should be reasonable and understandable to a U.S. Department of Labor auditor

If the employee holds more than one appointment title, you will need to complete a second Actual Wage Rate e-form with the other appointment title after completing this one

Return to "Department Services" home page and select the " Additional Actual Wage Rate for Multiple Appointment" request under TASKS.
