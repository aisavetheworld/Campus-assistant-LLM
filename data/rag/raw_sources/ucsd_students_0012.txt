How to Transfer to Another Department or Major (Graduates)

If you're a graduate student, follow these steps to transfer to another department or major.

1. Contact the department of destination.

Get approval from the department you are interested in transferring to .

2. Submit your request online.

Fill out and submit your request through the Change Major or Degree online form .
