UC SHIP Vision Plan

The UC Student Health Insurance Plan (UC SHIP) includes a comprehensive vision plan through Anthem Blue Cross called Blue View Vision. Your Anthem Blue Cross insurance card can also be used for the vision plan.

Visit UC SHIP for details regarding your benefits, including how to locate other participating providers. You may also call Anthem Blue Cross at (866) 940-8306.

The UCSD SHS Optometry Department is a participating provider for your Blue View Vision plan.
