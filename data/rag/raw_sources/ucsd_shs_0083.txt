Nutrition Services

During academic quarters, nutrition counseling is provided by our Registered Dietitians . This service includes nutrition counseling based on individual needs and follow-up visits as necessary. Topics covered include but are not limited to; general healthy eating, PCOS, GI conditions/concerns, eating disorders, food insecurity, and prediabetes.

In order to make an appointment with one of our dietitians, students need a referral from a primary care provider at Student Health Services, a psychiatrist at CAPS, or a Health Educator in Health Promotion Services. To make the most of the appointment, please fill out the UCSD Nutrition Assessment located within the e-check in process at least 24 hours in advance. Check-in is at the front desk on the first floor of Student Health.

There is NO FEE for registered students with UC SHIP and students with RAFT; students without UC SHIP or RAFT pay $20 per visit. See the Cost of Services page for more detail.

Nutrition Seminar Series

Join UCSD Student Health Services Registered Dietitians for a free virtual Nutrition Seminar Series this Winter Quarter.

Each one-hour webinar will take place from 12–1 PM via Zoom on the first Wednesday of each month , covering a different nutrition topic (see list below) and ending with a live Q&A.

Open to UCSD students only. Please register in advance using the links below. Recordings will be posted on the Student Health website after each session.

Meal Planning and Prepping – January 7, 2026 | 12–1 PM via Zoom Struggling to decide what to eat each week? Learn practical strategies to plan, shop, and prep simple, satisfying meals that fit your schedule, budget, and lifestyle.

Creating Balanced Meals and Snacks – February 4, 2026 | 12–1 PM via Zoom Discover how to build balanced, energizing meals and snacks by combining protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats. Ideal for busy students seeking quick, nourishing options.

Mindful Eating – March 4, 2026 | 12–1 PM via Zoom Explore how mindful eating can reduce overeating, improve digestion, and make meals more enjoyable. Learn easy ways to bring mindfulness into your daily routine.

Label Decoding – April 1, 2026 | 12–1 PM via Zoom Learn how to read and understand food labels to make informed choices and spot marketing traps. Gain confidence in navigating grocery aisles with ease.
