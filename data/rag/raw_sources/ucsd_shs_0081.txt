The Student Health Services Nurses’ Clinic supports student well-being with quick, convenient, and compassionate care, from vaccines to tuberculosis testing, guidance on filing out health requirement forms, medication administration, and more.

All Nurses’ Clinic services are by appointment only. Some appointments can be self-scheduled online (see table below) using MyStudentChart : Login > Menu > Schedule an Appointment > Choose “Nursing Immunization/TB/Forms Visit” or “SHS: Influenza/Tdap/HPV/COVID-19 Vaccines”.

For all other appointments (see table below), please call (858) 534-3300 to schedule.

There is no charge for Nurses’ Clinic visits, vaccines, and services for students covered by UC SHIP. RAFT students pay no fee for the visit but will pay out of pocket for the cost of vaccines & TB testing. Students without UC SHIP/RAFT will pay Fee for Service for the visit and any associated costs. See the Cost of Services page for more information.

Students seeking insurance reimbursement should always confirm with their provider whether reimbursement is available before being seen in clinic.

The Nurses' Clinic is open during regular clinic hours, including extended hours on Mondays until 6pm during the academic year, and closed on weekends and university holidays .

Online Support via MyStudentChart

For health-related questions, please login to MyStudentChart and choose Menu > Student Health Ask-A-Nurse to send a secure message directly to our nursing team. An RN will respond or forward the query to the appropriate department within 1- 2 business days, Monday to Friday, 8–4:30pm (see below for after-hours support).

24/7 Nurse Triage Phone Line

If you are currently sick or injured and need medical advice, call (858) 534-3300 to speak with a registered nurse who can provide guidance. This service is available 24/7 to all students at no cost regardless of insurance coverage.
