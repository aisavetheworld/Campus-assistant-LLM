Commuter Lounges

Spend your on-campus downtime at one of the 7 lounges designed for commuter students to eat, sleep, study, and relax.

Price Center Commuter Lounge (Level 1, PC East)

Central location near Starbucks and Food Court

Kitchen with hot-water dispensers for tea, etc.

Sofas, tables, and chairs

Eleanor Roosevelt (ERC) Commuter Lounge

Close to Glider Port parking

Peaceful environment with study tables

Long, comfy couches

Computer and printer

Refrigerator and sink

Gender neutral restrooms

Marshall Community Lounge

Coalition building, CB57 (basement level)

Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 11 p.m.

Muir Commuter Lounge “El Portal”

Just north of Middle of Muir (MOM) coffee and market

Study table and desks, including quiet room

Hydration station (hot and cold)

Commuter lockers for rent, quarterly

Revelle Commuter Lounge

Near 64 Degrees dining hall

Refrigerator and microwave

Comfy couches and study tables

Computers and printer

Sixth College Commuter Center

North Torrey Pines Living and Learning Neighborhood, Catalyst Building, basement level: The space can be accessed from the street level, adjacent to the Sixth College Market

Open 7 days a week, 8 a.m. to 9 p.m.

Wireless internet access, computers, and access to nearby printer

Kitchen facilities

Flat-screen TV and plenty of comfortable furniture

Commuter lockers for rent

Learn more on the Sixth College website

Warren Student Lounge

Located by the giant stone bear

Sink and hydration station

Chairs and tables
