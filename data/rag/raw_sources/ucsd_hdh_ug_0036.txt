Short-Term Transition Housing Info

UC San Diego Housing will remain open and operational for summer session. Students selecting to live on-campus for summer will be assigned to available space consisting of double and limited single bedrooms.  Application for Summer Housing will launch on April 13, 2026.

If you are a Fall 2026 incoming first year or incoming transfer student, we ask that you wait to submit a summer housing application and contract until you have a secured your campus AD account (after you've accepted your campus Admission and followed your checklist items).

Extended Housing

Extended Housing Process & Cost information below for those only participating in Summer Session 10 Week or 2nd 5 Week program with a 2026-27 academic year housing contract.

Duration of Program: September 6th, 2026 - September 15, 2026 (billed 9/6/26 - 9/15/26) Deadline to Add: TBD Deadline to Drop: Sunday, August 23rd, 2026 @ 11:59PM

Program Eligibility

Resident must be a regularly enrolled, full-time registered undergraduate student of the University as defined by the Registrar's Office, for the term of the 2026-27 academic year.

Resident m ust hold a UC San Diego Single Undergraduate Residential Contract for the 2026-27 academic year and hold a Summer Housing contract through September 6th.

If at any point the 2026-27 academic year contract is cancelled, they are no longer eligible or able to participate in the Extended Summer Housing Program.

Resident will be required to move into their fall contracted space on Wednesday, September 9th.

Students must move out of their current summer residence and be checked in to their regular academic space between 8:00am and 4:00pm on Wednesday, September 9th. To ensure a smooth transition each student will only be able to select a fall move in time during this time frame.

Students who are not physically here on campus on Wednesday, September 9th cannot contract for the extended housing period. There will be no exceptions.
