Changing Visa Status

If you are currently enrolled or have been admitted to UC San Diego under a different non-immigrant status (i.e., H4, E2, L2) and are considering changing your status to F-1 or J-1, please submit the email contact form to make an appointment with an international student advisor so that the timeline and documents can be reviewed.
