New International Student Orientation

The International Services and Engagement Office (ISEO) is excited to welcome you to UC San Diego! New International Student Orientation is designed to help orient you to the UC San Diego campus community, inform you of your F1/J1 rights & responsibilities, and give you an opportunity to connect with other new international students!

Please note that while ISEO programs are designed to serve international students, scholars, and their families, everyone in the Triton Community is welcome to participate regardless of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, disability, and/or other protected categories.
