Breast & Testicular Health

It is important for individuals to be familiar with their own breast s and breast tissue as they are the best person to detect changes. A breast self-exam is a step-by-step way to examine the breasts to know what is normal in order to recognize a change if one were to occur. If any of th e changes listed below a r e p r e s e n t , please see a medical provider as soon as possible for evaluation :

A lump or swelling

Skin irritation or dimpling

Nipple pain or the nipple turning inward

Redness or scaling of the nipple or breast skin

A discharge other than breast milk

Testicular Health

It is important for individuals to be familiar with their own testicles as s elf-examination is important for early detection of testicular cancer. Testicular cancer usually presents as a painless lump, swelling, or change in the size and/ or feel of a testicle. People with testicular cancer may also notice a feeling of heaviness or dull aching in the lower belly or scrotum. Early signs of testicular cancer are usually ( but not always) painless, so it’s important to see a medical provider for evaluation as soon as possible if any of these symptoms occur .

Appointments can be made with SHS by calling (858) 534-3300 or by using MyStudentChart . Walk-in services are also available through Urgent Care at SHS.
