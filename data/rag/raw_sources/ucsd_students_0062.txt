How to use this schedule

This schedule is not specific to individual academic calendar years.

Instead, this schedule shows a general pattern. The day and time for a final is based on a class' officially scheduled meeting time.

You can find dates for finals week on the current academic calendar .

As exceptions to this schedule may occur, to find the specific date of a final for a scheduled class, check individual course listings in the Schedule of Classes .

Building and room information will be available by Week 9 of each quarter. Check individual course listings in the Schedule of Classes .
