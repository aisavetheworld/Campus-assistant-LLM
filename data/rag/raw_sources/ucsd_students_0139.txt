Resources for Students with Dependents

Learn about UC San Diego housing options available to student couples and families.

UC San Diego housing is available for:

Full-time graduate and professional students (i.e., enrolled in 12 units or more throughout the lease term);

Graduate, professional, and undergrad students with children;

Full-time undergraduate student couples (enrolled in 12 units or more throughout the lease term); and

Staff and faculty
