After Graduation

Students who graduate in the Fall or Winter quarter will have their Student Health Insurance Plan (SHIP) coverage end on the last day of their graduating quarter. Students who are registered for and graduate in the Spring quarter will continue to receive SHIP coverage through the summer, with coverage ending on the first day of the Fall quarter for the following academic year. For the current coverage period, visit UC SHIP - Student Health Insurance Plan .
