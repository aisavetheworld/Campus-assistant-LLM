Returning after Gap Quarter(s)

Information about the process of returning to UC San Diego after taking one or more quarters away from a program. Students who are not eligible for reactivation must request a new Form I-20 or DS-2019.

Transferring a SEVIS Record to UC San Diego

Information about transferring a SEVIS record back to UC San Diego to complete a program. Students who are transferring their SEVIS record to start a new program must refer to instructions for New Students .

Returning from a Termination due to a Status Violation

UC San Diego students who have a terminated or completed SEVIS record are considered to be out of status. Learn how to regain F-1/J-1 status.
