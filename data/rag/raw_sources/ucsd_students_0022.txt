How to Drop a Class (Graduate Students)

Grad students, follow the timeline and see the steps for dropping a class. Choose from the list to see the appropriate steps:

When do you want to drop a class? Choose from the list to see the appropriate steps:

Before the start of the quarter, through Friday of Week 2

Use WebReg to drop the class. (You may not use WebReg to drop a class after the first 2 weeks of the quarter.)

You may not drop below 12 units if you hold an appointment at 50% time or are receiving a fellowship, traineeship, or scholarship.

If you are enrolled in fewer than 12 units, then you are eligible for a 25% time appointment at the discretion of your graduate department.

If you want to drop a class after the end of Week 2, you must receive approval from both your advisor/ department and the Division of Graduate Education and Postdoctoral Affairs (GEPA) .

To drop a class, you must submit a request through the Enrollment Authorization System (EASy)

Drops will be processed/completed by the Office of the Registrar. You will receive an email confirmation when the drop has been processed and you should check the accuracy of your enrollment in WebReg.

If you drop a class by the end of Week 4, it will not appear on your transcript.

If you drop a class after the end of Week 4, it will appear on your transcript with a "W" grade. A student may receive a maximum of one “W” per course.

Warning: Drops made after Week 2 are reviewed by GEPA for potential impact on financial support.

You are not permitted to drop a class after Week 9.

International Students: You must get approval to take fewer than 12 units. Contact the International Students & Programs Office , (858) 534-3730.

Questions? Contact the Graduate Division or the Registrar's Office .
