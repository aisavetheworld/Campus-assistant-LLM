Commuter Lockers

University Centers offers commuter students lockers that can be rented for the entire year, or by the quarter.

Locker locations:

Price Center East, Level 1 (near the Commuter Lounge)

Price Center East, Level 2 (near Green Hallway)

The Original Student Center, Level 1 (near Taco Villa)

Rent your locker at: lockit.ucsd.edu

Other locker locations:

Sixth College and Muir also offer commuter lockers through their Commuter Lounges.
