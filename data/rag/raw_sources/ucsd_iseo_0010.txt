Orientation Assistant Program

Volunteer Orientation Assistants (OAs), play an integral role during the New International Student Orientation held every September to welcome over 3,000 international students to UC San Diego.

Please note that while ISEO programs are designed to serve international students, scholars, and their families, everyone in the Triton Community is welcome to participate regardless of race, color, national origin, religion, sex, disability, and/or other protected categories.

Mandatory 2 hour OA Training

Signing up for one half day or a full day. A separate email will be sent out to sign up for times after you submit application below.

Responsibilities

Welcoming new international students

Meeting with students to determine their immediate needs

Checking students' immigration documents during Document Check Sessions

Providing information and materials on living in San Diego, such as bank accounts and drivers licenses

Assisting in locating short-term and long-term housing referrals in the area

Making campus and community office referrals as needed

Assisting with the Welcome Events, including International Student Welcome Reception

Skills and Qualifications

Highly motivated with a positive attitude

Excellent communication and problem-solving skills

Cross-cultural sensitivity, patience, and empathy

Ability to serve as role model and mentor
