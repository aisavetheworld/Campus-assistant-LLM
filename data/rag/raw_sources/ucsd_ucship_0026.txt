How to Use UC SHIP

Mental Health Services

UC SHIP includes benefits for mental or nervous disorders and substance abuse. A written referral from SHS or Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS) is required prior to all non-emergency treatment. You will incur some out-of-pocket expenses for services rendered outside SHS or CAPS. Please refer to the UC SHIP benefits booklet for benefit information.
