Rita Atkinson Residences

Rita Atkinson Residences

The Rita Atkinson Apartments are in a beautiful multi-story building with a min-gym, welcome lobby and relaxing outdoor courtyard. It is located near the UC San Diego School of Medicine within walking distance of the Club Med Cafe, shopping and restaurants, and features mini-double rooms with limited single rooms available. Apartments consist of singles and mini-doubles.
