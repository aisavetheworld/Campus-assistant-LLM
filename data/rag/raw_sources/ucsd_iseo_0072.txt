Preparing Your New Program

The International Services and Engagement Office (ISEO) is here to assist new F‑1 and J‑1 students with immigration matters. But we know there are a lot of things to prepare as you plan for your studies and life at UC San Diego as an international student ! Questions about housing, booking classes , or other campus services are best directed to the offices that manage those areas. ISEO has put together this page as a resource to connect you with other campus services and support your onboarding at UC San Diego.
