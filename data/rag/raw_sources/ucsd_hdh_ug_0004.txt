Housing Contract and Rates

In order to receive a housing offer and maintain the two-year housing guarantee, students must meet the admissions and housing deadlines. Admissions and housing deadlines are defined as follows:

Accept the offer of admission by the admissions deadline.

Set up your Active Directory (AD).

Activate your official ucsd.edu email.

Submit the housing application by the housing application deadline. The invite to apply for housing for those who completed steps 1-3. The email invite will be sent to your personal email on file and your ucsd.edu email. After the initial invite, all email reminders will be sent to your ucsd.edu email account.

The invite to apply for housing for those who completed steps 1-3.

The email invite will be sent to your personal email on file and your ucsd.edu email. After the initial invite, all email reminders will be sent to your ucsd.edu email account.

Sign the housing contract and submitting the $450 housing prepayment by the housing contract process deadline.

Move-in (pick up keys and check in) by first day of classes (unless approved for a late arrival).

Housing Application Process For Incoming Students

Students who have accepted their Offer of Admissions, activated their Single Sign On and activated their ucsd.edu email to UC San Diego may apply for housing in April. Please allow 48-72 hours after accepting the Offer of Admission, activating your Single Sign On and activating your ucsd.edu email to access the 2026-2027 Incoming Student Housing Application Process via the Housing Portal.

View your Enrollment Checklist for the next steps.

Students who accept their Offer of Admission, activate their Single Sign On and activate their ucsd.edu email will receive an email to start the Housing Application Process.

All housing applications/forms/templates/contracts are available in the Housing Portal .
