Part-Time Study (Undergraduate Students)

Part-Time Study (Undergraduate Students)

Find out if you're eligible for part-time status. If you're an undergraduate approved for part-time status, you may be eligible for reduced fees.

Definition: You are a part-time undergraduate student if you're approved to enroll for 10 or fewer units per quarter.

Note: No special approval is required to study part-time during the summer.

Eligibility and enrollment

You can be approved part-time status for reasons of: Employment Family responsibilities Medical Graduating senior status: May apply for only one of final three quarters of enrollment. Maximum of one quarter of part-time status will be approved for reason of graduating senior. International students in F-1 or J-1 visa status may apply in their final graduating term only.

Family responsibilities

Graduating senior status: May apply for only one of final three quarters of enrollment. Maximum of one quarter of part-time status will be approved for reason of graduating senior. International students in F-1 or J-1 visa status may apply in their final graduating term only.

Your college must grant approval for you to enroll as a part-time student.

You must reapply for part-time study either quarterly or yearly, depending on the application submitted.

International students in F-1 or J-1 visa status must be approved by the International Services and Engagement Office (ISEO), and may only be approved for Medical reason or Graduating senior status.

Application and approval

Complete the Part-Time Study Application (E-Form). Application deadline: By 11:59 PM PT on the Friday of Week 2 of the quarter, you must be enrolled in 10 units or fewer. If you've already paid full registration fees and you are later approved for part-time study, then your student account will be automatically credited the difference. If you'd like to request a refund for credit on your student account, contact Student Financial Solutions .

Approval for part-time study exempts students from the 36 unit-per-year minimum progress requirement.

Approval for part-time study is granted for one of the following: One full academic year (fall, winter and spring quarters) Two continuous quarters (fall and winter OR winter and spring) One single quarter (fall, winter or spring)

One full academic year (fall, winter and spring quarters)

Two continuous quarters (fall and winter OR winter and spring)

One single quarter (fall, winter or spring)

Approval is not automatic, and should not be assumed.

Note: If you're receiving financial assistance, be sure to contact the Financial Aid & Scholarships Office regarding eligibility requirements.

Note: Campus housing contracts require that students maintain full-time enrollment of a minimum of 12 units per quarter. If you live on campus and plan to enroll in less than 12 units, contact your Residence Life Office to request an exception.

Note: International students in F-1 or J-1 visa status may only be approved for Health or Final Quarter reasons. International students must receive additional authorization to drop below full time enrollment by submitting an International Student Reduced Course Load Request . You will receive an updated I-20 or DS-2019 with the reduced course load approval. Contact the International Services and Engagement Office with any questions regarding F-1 or J-1 status and reduced course load.

Change in status

If you drop to 10 units or fewer after Friday of Week 2 of classes, you will not receive a fee reduction.

If you receive a reduction in fees and you increase the number of units you're taking to 10.5 or more at any time in the quarter, you will be billed for the difference for full-time fees.

If you make any adjustments to your schedule Weeks 2 through 4, please check your student account. If you see that you have been incorrectly assessed full-time tuition, please contact the Office of the Registrar.

Any adjustments made after Week 4 could result in assessment of full-time fees. Courses dropped with a "W" grade are included in total enrolled units, including withdrawals from lab courses that may be adjusted later in the term.

Reduced fees and billing

If you have been approved for part-time study and are enrolled in 10 or fewer units by the Friday of Week 2, you are eligible for a reduction of:

One-half of tuition

One-half of nonresident supplemental tuition, if applicable

UC's Systemwide Tuition Stability Plan also known as Cohort Tuition

Beginning Fall 2022, tuition will be adjusted for each incoming undergraduate class but will subsequently remain flat until the student graduates, for up to six years.

Learn more about the Tuition Stability Plan and how it works at UC San Diego .
