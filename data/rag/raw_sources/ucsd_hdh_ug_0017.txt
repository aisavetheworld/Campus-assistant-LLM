Pangea Apartments & Residence Halls

Pangea Apartments & Residence Halls, formerly known as Marshall Residence Halls and Uppers, is a community of apartments for incoming and continuing transfer students and upper division undergraduate students. The neighborhood consists of furnished Residence Halls and Apartment units with single, double and triple accommodations. Residence Hall suites have a bathroom, living area, and patio/balcony. Apartments have a kitchen, bathroom, and living room. The community shares amenities such as study lounges, laundry facilities, and a volleyball and basketball halfcourt. Residents have access to dining at Oceanview Terrace, and Cafe Ventanas, the Bistro and Seventh Market are also a short walk away for dining, coffee, and shopping needs.
