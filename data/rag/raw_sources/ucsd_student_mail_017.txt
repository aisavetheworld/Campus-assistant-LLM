Rita Atkinson Residences

All letter mail and packages for the Rita Atkinson housing community are handled by the Student Mail team in partnership with the Rita Atkinson Residential Life team.

Letter mail and packages from USPS are delivered to Student Mail before being distributed to the Rita Atkinson Parcel Center within 1-2 days of receiving. All other carriers (i.e. UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, etc.) deliver directly to the Rita Atkinson team. During peak periods (i.e. move-in, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day, etc.), letter mail and packages may be delayed up to 2 business days.

Please use the information provided below to assist you with all of your Rita Atkinson mail and package needs.

Upon moving in to your on campus residence, you will be assigned a box number. The box number is used to ensure the Student Mail team can properly process your packages and letters. *Check your housing portal (QwikPass) or ask your Residential Life Office for your assigned box number.

Parcel Center Location & Hours

Hours : Check The Village page for hours.

Mailbox Location & Combinations

Location : The Rita Atkinson mailboxes are located outside the Rita Atkinson main entrance.

Hours : 24/7 Access

Please contact your Residence Life Office for your mailbox combination.

How to Pick Up a Package at Rita Atkinson

Receive an email from studentmail@ucsd.edu.

Visit the Rita Atkinson Parcel Center.

Present your Student ID and box number to the parcel center staff.

Receive package.

How to Open Your Mailbox at Rita Atkinson

Turn LEFT 3 times and stop at the 1st number.

Turn RIGHT one full turn and stop at the 2nd number.

Turn LEFT to the 3rd number. Pull to open!

NOTE: Locks may need to be pulled to the right slightly after 3rd number to open.

Students can send outgoing packages and letter mail at The Trove , located on Library Walk. The Trove is a full-service postal annex for all of your shipping and mailing needs. At The Trove, you can ship packages, purchase stamps or shipping supplies, and drop off prepaid USPS, UPS, or FedEx packages.
