Registered Dietitians

In order to make an appointment with one of our dietitians, you will need a referral from your primary care provider at Student Health Services, your psychiatrist at CAPS, or a Health Educator in Health Promotion Services.

May Rees Registered Dietitian (858) 534-9544 May Rees (she/her/hers) earned her B.S. in Clinical Nutrition from University of California, Davis. After moving to San Diego, she went on to pursue an M.S. in Nutrition for Wellness at Bastyr University, California, and then her dietetic internship. She spent the first few years of her career working in the adolescent eating disorder treatment setting before joining UCSD Student Health in 2020. May focuses on providing weight-inclusive care with the goal of focusing on health-promoting behaviors. She is also passionate about integrating principles of intuitive eating and maintaining a holistic perspective in her work with clients. In her free time, you can find May enjoying live music, gardening, traveling, cuddling with her dog, and spending time in the sunshine.

Jenna Richter Registered Dietitian (858) 822-0085 Jenna Richter (she/her/hers) received her B.S. in Nutrition Science from California State University Northridge. She then completed her dietetic internship and Master's in Dietetics from Iowa State University. Jenna spent the first few years of her career in the inpatient, and long-term care settings in San Diego. Since 2020, and up until joining the UCSD Student Health team in 2025, Jenna worked with both adults and adolescents struggling with eating disorders. Jenna believes in progress over perfection, and that food is not just fuel; it can be a source of joy, cultural connection, and satisfaction to support both mental and physical well-being. In her spare time, Jenna enjoys reading in the sunshine, yoga, travelling, and perfecting her chocolate chip cookie recipe.

Elizabeth von Klan Registered Dietitian (858) 822-0085 Elizabeth von Klan, MS, RDN (she/her/hers) earned her Bachelor of Science in Clinical Nutrition from the University of California, Davis, and her Master of Science in Nutrition, Healthspan, and Longevity from the University of Southern California. She completed her dietetic internship across diverse clinical and community-based sites throughout California, with concentrated experience on the Central Coast. Elizabeth’s professional background includes work in school-based nutrition and food service programs, as well as residential treatment settings for eating disorders. She currently serves in a dual role with Recreation and Student Health Services, where she provides individualized nutrition counseling and education to both UC San Diego students and staff. She also serves as the Registered Dietitian for the UCSD WorkStrong Program . Her clinical approach prioritizes sustainable behavior change and incorporates a “Nutrition by Addition” framework to promote balanced, flexible eating patterns and long-term nutritional well-being. Her work is grounded in fostering positive relationships with food while supporting overall health outcomes. Outside of work, Elizabeth enjoys exploring coffee shops, visiting the zoo, scrapbooking, and attending concerts.
