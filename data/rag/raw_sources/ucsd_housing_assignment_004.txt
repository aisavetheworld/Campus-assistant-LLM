Undergrad Waitlist

The Undergraduate Waitlist open on a quarterly basis.  Students need to apply each quarter they are in need of housing.  Contracts are offered based on date in which you applied, the gender of roommates in the space available, available spaces and room type, and the location of the vacancy.  Students are responsible for updating their information through the waitlist form.  If you are no longer in need of housing, please use the button below to login and remove yourself from the waitlist.

Understanding the Undergraduate Waitlist

Fall 2026 Quarter (Closed):

Waitlist Opens: May 11, 2026* Waitlist Closes: October 26, 2026

*Only available for current students.  Fall 2026 admitted students should not be getting on the waitlist when it opens as our application and housing process will still be active.  Check out the Important Dates for incoming students. Getting on the waitlist does not guarantee a space on campus.  We will make offers once there are available space for the 2026-27 academic year.

Winter 2026 Quarter:

Waitlist Opens: November 4, 2025 Waitlist Closes: February 16, 2026 at 11:59pm (CLOSED)

Update 2/24/2026: If you are looking for on-campus housing for Winter Quarter, please reach out to us at ucsdhousing@ucsd.edu with your PID and name.  We have vacancies in triple rooms and limited double/mini-double rooms at the moment. Information on rates can be found here: 2025-26 UG Housing Rates

Spring 2026 Quarter:

Waitlist Opens: February 23, 2026 Waitlist Closes: May 8, 2026 at 11:59am

UG Waitlist closes at 11:59am on May 8.  If you need housing for the remainder of Spring Quarter, please contact ucsdhousing@ucsd.edu with your name and PID.

Off Campus Resource:

The number one resource is the Off-Campus Housing website , a resource available only to UC San Diego affiliates with a single sign-on account. The site features current listings, house hunting tips and a roommate finder tool.

Students can also join the Facebook group for the Associated Students Office of Food and Housing Resources to connect with other students who need a roommate or who may have a room, apartment or house to rent.

Another helpful housing guide can be found on the Office of Food & Housing Resources website.
