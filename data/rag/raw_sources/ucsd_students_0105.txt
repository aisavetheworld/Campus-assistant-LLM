Off-Campus Rental Forms

Access forms that will help you when renting an off-campus property.

Student Legal Services has sample forms available for you to use when moving off campus. Please consult Student Legal Services for advice and questions regarding contracts and leases.

Roommate Agreement : You may want to use a formal roommate agreement that clearly outlines expenses and responsibilities.

Rental Property Inventory Checklist : Before taking possession of your new home, you may want to document the condition of the property and inventory items on site.

Sublease Agreement : If you plan to sublease your residence, you'll want to help protect yourself by using a formal agreement.
