F-1 and J-1 Visa Eligibility

International students who want to study full-time at UC San Diego must have a valid I-94 status for the duration of their program. Most students apply for F-1 student status or J-1 Exchange Visitor student status. UC San Diego will issue a student an I-20 form (F-1) or a DS-2019 form (J-1) depending on their eligibility. While most international degree-seeking students study on the F-1 Visa, certain students, such as our University of California Education Abroad Program (UCEAP) and Sponsored Students are typically on the J-1 Visa. Sponsored Students : A sponsored student is a student whose tuition and fees are paid directly to UC San Diego by an outside private or public agency, such as a corporation, government, educational institution or embassy. Sponsored students typically receive a DS-2019 form (J-1). Sponsored students who wish to receive the I-20 form and study on the F-1 v isa must obtain a letter from their sponsor explaining why the F-1 visa is the appropriate student visa category for the student’s objectives and indicating their support of the student's F-1 visa application.
