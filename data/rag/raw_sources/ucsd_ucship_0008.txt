Health Fee and Insurance Requirement Virtual Info Sessions

Join us for the UC San Diego Health Insurance Requirement & Access to Care on Campus Webinar Series. These educational webinars are designed to help students and families better understand the University health insurance requirement, UC SHIP, the health fee waiver process, RAFT, and how to access care at Student Health Services and the new Triton Center.

There will be 10 webinar offerings available throughout the summer. Students and families are encouraged to attend one session, as each webinar will cover the same information. Sessions will also include a live Q&A opportunity to ask questions directly to the Student Health Insurance team. A recording will be uploaded afterward for those unable to attend live.

Whether you are new to UC San Diego or would simply like a refresher, these webinars are a great way to prepare for the upcoming academic year and learn more about the health and well-being resources available on campus.
