Getting Involved in Community

Residential Community Organizers (RCOs)

Our Residential Community Organizers are a group of resident-leaders who engage our community through events and programming across our four Upper Division Housing areas. Looking to join the team? Keep an eye out for Spring recruitment posts on the RCO instagram: @rcoinfo

Residential Assistants (RAs)

Our Residential Assistants each serve a residential community within our for Upper Division Housing areas through community engagement, programming, and residential support. Want to be an RA? Recruitment information will become available to interested students prior to Winter Quarter.

Programs and Events

Upper Division Housing hosts a variety of programs and events throughout the year, including movie nights, quarterly large-scale events, and finals week tradition: Unwind Your Mind . Check with your RA and the Upper Division Housing Program Calendar below to see what’s coming up next!
