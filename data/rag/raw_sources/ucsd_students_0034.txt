How to Enroll in Classes at CSU Campuses

If you're a UC San Diego undergraduate, find out how you can take classes at a California State University (CSU) campus through the Cross Enrollment Program.

1. Complete a cross enrollment form.

Contact the admissions office at the CSU campus you plan to attend, and request a cross enrollment form. Provide the information requested.

2. Get verification of your eligibility.

Take the cross enrollment form to the UCSD Registrar's Office , Student Services Center ( map ) for completion of the verification portion of the form.

3. Submit the form.

Submit the completed form to the appropriate CSU registrar's office.
