How to Fulfill the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement

Find out how to meet the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement.

What is the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR)?

The University of California requires all undergraduate students to demonstrate a minimum proficiency in English composition by meeting the Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR). This is one of the undergraduate degree requirements .

You must fulfill the ELWR before you are able to enroll in university-level writing courses.

You must fulfill the ELWR within a limited time frame.

How do I meet the ELWR?

Prior to enrollment at UC San Diego, you may meet the requirement with a qualifying SAT, ACT, AP, or IB test score, or a transferable college composition course. Visit the ELWR page for details.

What if I don't have a qualifying score or course?

You must complete a writing placement exam in summer.

If the exam places you in College Writing courses, you have fulfilled the ELWR.

If the exam places you in AWP 3 or AWP 4A-4B, you must take the course during your first quarter at UC San Diego. You will fulfill the ELWR when you pass AWP 3 or AWP 4B with a C or better.
