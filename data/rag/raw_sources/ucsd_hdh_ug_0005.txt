Housing Contract

Living on campus at UC San Diego is an incredible opportunity to connect with your community, engage in campus life, and benefit from a supportive living and learning environment. When you choose university housing, you enter into a formal agreement that outlines important details about your residency, including contract terms, cancellation policies, and the termination process.

To ensure you’re fully informed, we’ve provided everything you need to know—just scroll down for details on housing contracts, key dates, cancellation policies, fees, and more. If you have questions, our team is here to help!

We’re excited to support you in making the most of your on-campus experience.

After completing the housing contract process (but prior to check-in and picking up keys or prior to your rental agreement start date, whichever occurs first), if you need to cancel your housing contract, you may do so by logging into the Housing Portal and filling out a cancellation form. Depending on when you choose to cancel your housing contract, a cancellation fee may be assessed.

It's important to note that once you have picked up keys or reached your contract start date , you are no longer eligible to "cancel" per your housing contract and the cancellation policy, instead follow the Contract Termination Process (see below).

If you cancel or terminate your current academic year housing contract you will forfeit your current and future housing guarantee. If you would like to live on campus in the future, you may apply for our housing waitlist.

Contract Terminations

Review your housing contract (Section 8.a. Termination by Student) for termination criteria. You are fully responsible for all housing and dining charges until a decision is rendered on your termination request and (if approved) you complete the move out process.

If a student meets the termination criteria to terminate their contract, they can log into the Housing Portal and submit a Contract Termination Request form.

Termination Requests that do not meet the housing contract criteria will go through the following process:

The termination request is submitted via the Housing Portal and reviewed along with any accompanying documentation by Housing Administration Services team members.

If the request meets the housing termination contract criteria and has appropriate supporting documentation,  it will be approved and a decision will be sent to the student via email. You should not move out and return keys until you receive a final decision.

If the termination does not meet the housing contract termination criteria, the student may receive one of the decisions below: Referral : The reason and/or supporting documentation did not meet the termination criteria as defined in their housing contract. Student receives a referral to resources which may include but are not limited to: Room Change Interest List, Residential Life Office, Counseling and Psychological Services, Office for Students with Disabilities, or other campus resources for assistance. Missing Documentation : The supporting documentation provided did not support the termination reason and/or was not sufficient. Student has the opportunity to upload additional documents via the Housing Portal. Denied : The reason and/or supporting documentation did not meet the termination criteria as defined in their housing contract.

Referral : The reason and/or supporting documentation did not meet the termination criteria as defined in their housing contract. Student receives a referral to resources which may include but are not limited to: Room Change Interest List, Residential Life Office, Counseling and Psychological Services, Office for Students with Disabilities, or other campus resources for assistance.

Missing Documentation : The supporting documentation provided did not support the termination reason and/or was not sufficient. Student has the opportunity to upload additional documents via the Housing Portal.

Denied : The reason and/or supporting documentation did not meet the termination criteria as defined in their housing contract.

If you disagree with the termination decision, you can request to have the Undergraduate Housing Committee review your appeal and documentation. Please note that due to available meeting dates and times, decisions for terminations can take a few weeks, please plan accordingly .

The request will be reviewed by the Undergraduate Housing Committee. Voting members of the committee are made up of Residential Life Residential Assistants and/or Housing Assistants, Residential Life professional(s), and a Basic Needs representative.  The Chair of the Committee is the Associate Director of Housing Administrative Services and functions as a non-voting member.

The student will be notified of the Committee's decision via email.

Once a decision is rendered on an appeal, it is considered final .  The appeal cannot be submitted a second time for review.  A student wishing to provide new information pertaining to their case after a decision has been made, must do so within 10 business days of the original decision.

NOTE: If a Contract Termination Request is granted, the housing costs and dining dollars will be pro-rated based on when the student completes the move-out process which includes an approved Contract Termination, any residential housing move-out requirements and returning keys to the Residence Life Office. Any Dining Dollar or Triton Cash over-usage will be charged to the student, see below.

Over Usage of Dining Plan Funds

Dining Dollars and Triton Cash associated with your housing contract are intended to support your dining needs throughout the full academic year.  It's important to make sure you are properly budgeting your funds throughout the academic year. A monthly reminder to check your dining plan funds is sent to your ucsd.edu email.

If you are approved for contract termination, your housing and dining costs will be prorated to reflect your final check out date. If you have used more Dining Dollars or Triton Cash than what is allocated for that period (based on a daily rate), you may be charged for the overuse (you have spent more funds than paid for).

Please contact Triton Card Account Services to understand how early termination may impact your Dining Plan any applicable over use charges.

If you have any questions regarding your Dining Dollar or Triton Cash balances, please contact Triton Card Accounts Services via Service Now at https://support.ucsd.edu/students or call us at 858.534.4010.

Contract Termination Submittal Deadlines

You may contact Housing Administrative Services via Service Now if you have an extenuating circumstance that prevented you from submitting your termination request by the deadline. It’s important to note that we are closed during the Campus Winter Curtailment period and may not respond to termination inquiries until campus operations resume in January.

End of Fall Quarter 2026

Contract terminations must be submitted by 12/10/26 and those approved must complete the official check-out process on or before 12/12/26 (the last day of finals for Fall Quarter). Students who wish to submit a contract termination request after 12/10/26 will not be able to access the termination request module in the Housing Portal until campus operations resume 1/4/27.

Students submitting a termination request on or after 1/4/27, regardless of their requested move out date or official check out date, will be financially responsible for the first two weeks of Winter Quarter.

End of Winter Quarter 2027

Contract terminations must be submitted by 3/18/27 and those approved must complete the check-out process on or before 3/19/27.

Students submitting a termination request on or after 3/18/27, regardless of their requested move out date or official check out date, will be financially responsible for the first two weeks of Spring Quarter.

End of Spring Quarter 2027

Contract terminations must be submitted by 5/27/27 and those approved must complete the check-out process on or before 5/28/27 to avoid being charged to the end of the quarter.

Students who choose to move out between 5/29/27 – 6/12/27 do not need to submit a termination and should complete their express check-out process to avoid improper check-out fees. Students moving out from 5/26/27 – 6/12/27 are financially responsible for the last two weeks of Spring Quarter regardless of their check out date.
