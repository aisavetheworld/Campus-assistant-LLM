Welcome to TritonGPT: AI-Powered Support

TritonGPT changes the way you access information and resources at UC San Diego. Whether you're looking for quick answers, tailored advice, or help navigating UC San Diego's vast resources, TritonGPT is your go-to platform.

What is TritonGPT?

TritonGPT is UC San Diego’s AI platform, hosted securely on campus infrastructure at the San Diego Supercomputer Center. Designed specifically for our campus community, TritonGPT leverages the latest AI technology to provide fast, accurate, and personalized support.

Why use TritonGPT?

Fast and Reliable: Get instant answers to your questions, 24/7.

UCSD-Specific: TritonGPT is trained on UC San Diego’s unique resources and policies, ensuring accurate and relevant responses.

Secure and Private: Hosted on campus infrastructure, your data stays safe and secure.

Tips for getting the most out of TritonGPT

Be Specific : The more detailed your prompt, the better TritonGPT can assist you.

Explore Use Cases : Use TritonGPT for brainstorming ideas, summarizing content, or even drafting emails and presentations.

Verify Sources: Always review the source links provided in responses for additional context and to confirm information directly.

Provide Feedback : Click thumbs-up or thumbs-down and share comments after receiving responses, and help TritonGPT improve.
