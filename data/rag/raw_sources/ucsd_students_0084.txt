Graduate Programs

Graduate School Preparation Programs

These undergraduate enrichment programs are designed to help you prepare for education beyond a bachelor's degree.

UC San Diego Grad Division programs:

Summer Training Academy for Research Success (STARS) : An 8-week summer research academy for undergraduate students and recent college graduates, open to all disciplines.

Summer Training Academy for Research Success (STARS) : An 8-week summer research academy for undergraduate students and recent college graduates, open to all disciplines.

University of California Leadership Excellence through Advanced Degrees Program (UC LEADS) : This program offers support for upper-division undergraduates majoring in science, engineering, or mathematics who want to pursue a Ph.D.

University of California Leadership Excellence through Advanced Degrees Program (UC LEADS) : This program offers support for upper-division undergraduates majoring in science, engineering, or mathematics who want to pursue a Ph.D.

GradAMP : A free mentorship program that makes graduate education more accessible by helping undergrads with the application process (for upper-division undergraduates majoring in science, engineering, or mathematics who want to pursue a Ph.D.).

GradAMP : A free mentorship program that makes graduate education more accessible by helping undergrads with the application process (for upper-division undergraduates majoring in science, engineering, or mathematics who want to pursue a Ph.D.).

Other campus programs:

Faculty Mentor Program : Juniors and seniors with a GPA of 2.7 or higher can work with faculty mentors on an individual basis.

Faculty Mentor Program : Juniors and seniors with a GPA of 2.7 or higher can work with faculty mentors on an individual basis.

Health Beat : This centralized pre-med and pre-health advising office at the Career Center offers strategic planning, 4-year plans, resources, events, classes and more to help you become a competitive applicant to medical school or other health professional schools (i.e., pharmacy, dentistry, physician assistant (PA), nursing, occupational therapy/ physical therapy, veterinary medicine, public health and more).

Health and Medical Professions Mentorship Program (HMP3) : All students interested in careers in the health professions, including medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, and other fields may enroll in this student-run program.

Health and Medical Professions Mentorship Program (HMP3) : All students interested in careers in the health professions, including medicine, dentistry, pharmacy, and other fields may enroll in this student-run program.

McNair Program : Low-income, first-generation college students and underrepresented minorities can apply for this year-long preparation for doctoral studies.

McNair Program : Low-income, first-generation college students and underrepresented minorities can apply for this year-long preparation for doctoral studies.

Medical Education for Diverse Students (MEDS) : An all-day conference for pre-med, pre-pharmacy, pre-health students at UC San Diego and neighboring community colleges.
