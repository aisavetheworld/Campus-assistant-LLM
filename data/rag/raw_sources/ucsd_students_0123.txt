Student Mail supports 17 housing communities across UC San Diego with dedicated parcel centers located near or within a student resident’s housing community. Our team receives, processes, and distributes packages from all major carriers including but not limited to USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL, Amazon, and USPS letter mail.

We aim to provide a seamless, timely, and student-centered mail experience. Whether you're expecting a letter from home, an online order, or official university communications, Student Mail is here to ensure fast, reliable, and convenient access to your mail and packages with minimal hassle.

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is to ensure fast, secure, and reliable delivery of mail and packages to the on-campus resident population. We are committed to excellence, providing friendly and efficient service that supports the UC San Diego community. We strive to be the trusted leader in student mail services, enhancing the student experience through seamless, accessible, and innovative solutions that drive logistics excellence
